Standings

Luck = actual wins minus expected wins. Elo is an opponent-adjusted strength rating (1500 is average). Pennant Score (0 to 100) blends results, underlying strength, and outlook. 1-run: record in one-run games, with ↓ for an unusually good record likely to slip and ↑ for an unusually poor one likely to improve. Home RD and Road RD: run differential at home and away. vs .500+: record against teams above .500. Click a column to sort. Details are in How it works.
